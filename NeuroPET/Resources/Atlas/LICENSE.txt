Licenses of the atlas files in this folder
==========================================

These files come with the extension so that the Neuro workflows run without a download. They are not covered by the
extension's own license (MIT, see LICENSE at the top of the repository): each keeps the license of its source, given
below. Layout: Space/<space id>/ (template volumes, space.json) and Segmentation/<segmentation id>/ (labels,
tables, segmentation.json). The volumes are stored as NRRD (gzip): the voxel values of the originals unchanged, only
the file format converted (the grey matter map as float, its NIfTI scaling applied); otherwise as noted.

3. CerebrA segmentations made for this extension
-------------------------------------------------

Files (Segmentation/CerebrA/):
    labels.seg.nrrd, labels.csv, segmentation.json

The CerebrA labels (2) with the pons added as a structure of its own, inside the brainstem, by the extension's
author; the rest of the brainstem as "Brainstem (except pons)", no optic chiasm, segments named after the
structures. A derivative of CerebrA; the addition is given under the same terms (CC0 1.0). Cite CerebrA (2).

Files (Segmentation/CerebrA_PET/):
    labels.seg.nrrd, labels.csv, segmentation.json

CerebrA PET, regions for PET made by the extension's author from the CerebrA with pons above: the right caudate
edited by hand and the left one mirrored from it; cortical regions grown 3 mm, the cingulate, insula, basal ganglia,
thalamus, amygdala and hippocampus 2 mm into the white matter and the unlabelled brain (inside the MNI brain mask;
brainstem, pons, cerebellum and diencephalon not grown; ventricles never taken). A derivative of CerebrA, given under
the same terms (CC0 1.0). Cite CerebrA (2).

groups.csv and groups_compatible.csv in both segmentation folders (the broad regions) and the space.json /
segmentation.json manifests are part of the extension (MIT).


4. Chinese Brain PET Template (CNPET) - not in this folder
----------------------------------------------------------

No CNPET file comes with the extension. Normal databases shared for the Neuro workflows (mean / SD maps, regional
statistics and surface projections made with the Neuro PET Database Manager) may be built from the normal [18F]FDG
brain PET images of this dataset; its license is kept here for them. Such databases carry the license and the
citation in their own description too (database.json), shown wherever the database is picked.

Source: https://www.nitrc.org/projects/cnpet ; https://github.com/DlutMedimgGroup/Chinese-Brain-PET-Template ;
dataset DOI: 10.25790/bml0cm.95
